About Strong Enough to Speak

Strong Enough to Speak is an emerging men's mental-health and wellness initiative focused on awareness, peer connection, healthy coping, early help-seeking and appropriate professional referral.

Founded by Clayton T. Banda

Clayton T. Banda is a Health Psychology student at Midlands State University and a Certified Peer Educator and Peer Counsellor.

"You don't have to face everything alone."

```
```

Our Mission

To promote men's mental and emotional wellbeing by creating safe spaces for conversation, connection, education, peer support and access to appropriate professional services.

Our Programmes

Our activities are designed to make conversations about men's mental health more accessible and supportive.

🧠 Mental Health Awareness

Educational sessions covering mental health, depression, anxiety, stress, trauma, substance-use concerns, healthy coping and help-seeking.

🗣️ Men's Talk Circles

Structured conversations where men can discuss topics such as relationships, stress, masculinity, academic pressure, family responsibilities and emotional wellbeing.

🤝 Peer Support

Peer-based listening, encouragement and emotional support within clearly defined peer-support boundaries.

⚽ Sport & Wellness

Sport, physical activity and recreational activities designed to encourage healthy routines and positive social connection.

🎓 Campus Outreach

Mental-health awareness campaigns, discussions, workshops and wellness activities for students.

🏘️ Community Outreach

Community-based mental-health awareness and men's wellness activities developed through partnerships.

🏥 Professional Referral

Building relationships with qualified professionals and established organisations so participants can access appropriate specialised support.
